# Case: Specification Conflicts with Brainstorm

Input: a draft specification includes Digital Products and Group Buying in MVP;
brainstorm recommends Digital Products only. No authorized decision exists.

Expected:

1. Both claims and sources are recorded; neither is silently discarded.
2. Draft specification and brainstorm remain below explicit user decisions and
   approved lifecycle artifacts in authority.
3. Impact on scope, requirements, UI, architecture and delivery is stated.
4. The agent recommends a resolution but leaves the conflict unresolved.
5. Downstream approval is blocked when the choice materially changes MVP scope.
