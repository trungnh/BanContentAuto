# Conduct Brownfield Change

Use for bug fixes, CRs, features, and major evolution after an approved baseline.

1. Load `.agents/skills/aidlc-change/SKILL.md` and follow it completely.
2. Put the supplied ticket/change identity and authoritative input in
   `project/change.md`; do not replace missing business decisions with assumptions.
3. Run the current lifecycle directive through the regular `/aidlc` conductor.
4. Do not implement until classification and plan gates are approved.
